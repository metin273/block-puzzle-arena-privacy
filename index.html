English

This policy covers Block Puzzle Arena by Metin Öztürk Software. Legacy editions use Google Play Games leaderboards or optional developer-operated rankings as described below. The forthcoming Arena competition update is not yet released; its separate disclosures apply only to editions showing the ARENA welcome and participation flow. Contact metinzr55@gmail.com for support and privacy requests.

Forthcoming Arena competition edition

Competition and public rankings are core functionality in this edition. Before playing, sign in with Google Play Games, complete your profile and actively accept the game participation rules. If you decline, no new online player profile is created and you can leave the app. Advertising choices and Google friends permissions remain separate and optional. A privacy notice acknowledgement is not blanket consent to unrelated processing.

First and last name are separate profile fields, are not public, and need not be legal names. No identity document or proof of real identity is requested. Choose a safe public player name, especially for younger players. Only that display name, self-selected country and verified scores are shown in rankings. Name, account identifiers, country, rules acceptance version/time, verified record, friend requests, blocks, and wallet transactions are held in Cloudflare Workers/D1. The same verified Google game account restores the same profile after reinstalling or changing devices. Unverified local scores remain on the device and are not presented as accepted public records.

New managed wallets store their opening grant, verified rewards and debits in an idempotent server ledger. Existing local wallets remain intact pending an individually reviewed migration; arbitrary client balances are never accepted. When enabled, AdMob server verification receives an opaque account identifier and one-time reward reference, not first/last name or board history. Reward claims are accepted only after signature verification. Google-derived friend links expire within 30 days and can be refreshed only through the permission flow; in-game code friendships require mutual acceptance. You may remove or block friends. Denying Google friends access does not prevent code friendships.

Required account/game processing must have an applicable legal basis for providing the requested competitive service; optional advertising and social permissions are not prerequisites. Applicability of child/parental permission and international transfer requirements depends on age and jurisdiction; this notice does not replace those requirements. Children should use safe pseudonyms and review the notice with their guardian. No personal names appear in public rankings.

For this edition, Settings → Arena and privacy → Delete my account and data removes the online account, scores, friendships, blocks, wallet ledger, reward intents and account-owned migration records. The legacy opt-out/local-wallet-preservation descriptions below concern older editions. Use the web deletion contact below if the app is unavailable. Account-owned data is kept until deletion, except the shorter periods described below; recovery history may remain for up to seven days. This edition does not promise cross-device restoration of an unfinished board: gameplay resumes from the saved device record.

Legacy editions: information stored on your device

The game stores your chosen player name and country, a randomly generated player identifier, your personal best score, game statistics, board and pieces, move history, current score, and sound, haptic and motion preferences. These records are used to display your profile, resume your game and remember your settings. Choosing a country does not give the app access to your physical location.

Legacy editions: collection, sharing and online services

We do not sell your data. Google Play Games remains the sign-in provider. In legacy versions, Google processes your submitted scores and selected country for its leaderboards under your Google visibility settings. In versions supporting our optional online rankings, we operate a separate ranking service using Cloudflare Workers and D1. Choosing to participate authorizes the service to process your verified game-scoped Google player identifier, chosen name and country, random game/account identifiers, legal game moves and board state, scores, timestamps, friendship requests/connections and participation preferences. Authentication codes are exchanged with Google on the server. Google OAuth access tokens are used transiently, not stored in our database. Passwords, Google email addresses and Google profile photos are not collected by this ranking service.

Online ranking names, chosen countries and accepted scores are shown to other players who use the ranking service. Google Play Games activity need not be public. Country is self-selected, not verified physical location. Friend codes and requests enable mutually approved in-game friendships. Google friends are imported only after the supported Google permission flow and server verification; names/countries of a request sender are shown to the recipient so they can decide. You can decline participation and continue playing offline. Older local scores are not silently uploaded as verified records. Game progress and diamonds remain on your device; the ranking database is not a cloud-save backup. The game also checks Google Play for updates using the official immediate update flow.

Cloudflare processes HTTPS connections and infrastructure/security data to provide the service. The API temporarily stores a hash of the connection IP for login rate limiting; we do not store raw IP addresses in the game database. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy. Our Worker request-body logging is disabled. Infrastructure providers may process their own operational/security records under their policies.

Advertising and consent

The Android release includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Only a small banner at the bottom of game screens and optional rewarded advertisements to earn one diamond or continue a lost game are used. Ads may be unavailable while the app is awaiting advertising approval or when a connection or ad is unavailable. The game remains playable without them. No interstitial or app-open ads are requested.

Google may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and device identifiers, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The Android advertising ID permission is included with the advertising SDK; this release applies Google's under-age-of-consent treatment to all ad requests, disables personalized advertising and remarketing, and limits ad content to PG. We do not collect your age or transmit your local player name, wallet, board or move history to the advertising service. Optional Play Games score and country processing is described above. The app does not request contacts, camera, microphone or precise location permissions. Country selection is separate from approximate location derived from a network connection.

UMP checks applicable privacy requirements before the advertising SDK is initialized or ads are loaded. When required, a consent form is shown and an Advertising privacy entry appears in Settings to review choices. If consent cannot be established, ads are not loaded. Google encrypts SDK network data in transit using TLS; this does not mean local game records are encrypted. Read Google's privacy policy and how Google uses advertising data. Google controls retention of the data it processes; we cannot specify or remotely erase those records. Android privacy settings allow you to manage or delete your advertising ID.

If you choose to open this policy or contact us, your browser, GitHub Pages and email provider process the connection or message under their own policies. GitHub may log technical information when serving this website. See GitHub's privacy statement. This website contains no developer-operated analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies.

Retention, deletion and requests

Game records remain in the app's device storage until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Starting a new game replaces the current board but keeps your profile and personal best. To remove the app's records, use Android Settings → Apps → Block Puzzle Arena → Storage → Clear data. Device backup or restore behavior, if available, is controlled by Android and your device settings.

In versions offering online rankings, use Settings → Ranking privacy → Delete my online data to permanently remove your developer-operated online profile, ranking scores, game tickets/state, sessions, friend requests and friendship connections after signing in to the same Play Games account. This preserves local game records and diamonds. Turning participation off removes public ranking entries but retains your private profile and verified personal-best summary until account deletion; rejoining starts new ranking entries. Clearing app storage alone does not delete online data. If you cannot access the app, email metinzr55@gmail.com with a deletion request. We will verify account control before deleting records; do not send passwords or authentication tokens. Google-managed Play Games records are separate: use Google’s deletion instructions.

Profiles, verified all-time scores and approved friendships are retained until withdrawal/deletion as described above. Hourly cleanup removes expired app sessions (one-hour lifetime), expired rate-limit windows (normally minutes to two hours), unanswered friend requests after 30 days, game tickets/state after their 24-hour lifetime plus seven days, daily score buckets older than eight days and weekly buckets older than five weeks. Daily/weekly periods reset at 00:00 UTC and Monday 00:00 UTC respectively; they use scores accepted in that period. Cloudflare D1 Free may retain recoverable database history for seven days; deleted data in this history ages out automatically and is not used for public rankings. Support messages are retained as needed to resolve requests and meet obligations. Provider-managed Google/ad data follows the provider’s retention policies.

Security and children

The app uses the device's application storage. We do not claim that game files are encrypted or that device storage is immune to unauthorized access or loss. This version does not offer messaging, purchases or gambling. It is intended for a general teen and adult audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13.

Changes

We will update the effective date when this policy changes. The behavior and permissions of the version installed on your device determine which features are available.
