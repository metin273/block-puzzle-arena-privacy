English

This policy describes the current Android version of Block Puzzle Arena, published by Metin Öztürk Software. Contact us at metinzr55@gmail.com for support or privacy questions.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your chosen player name and country, a randomly generated player identifier, your personal best score, game statistics, board and pieces, move history, current score, and sound, haptic and motion preferences. These records are used to display your profile, resume your game and remember your settings. Choosing a country does not give the app access to your physical location.

Collection, sharing and online services

In this version, the developer does not collect or receive these game records from your device and does not sell or share them with third parties. Online rankings, Google Play Games sign-in and cloud profile synchronization are not active. A player profile in the game is an on-device profile, not an online account.

The game does not request advertising identifiers, contacts, camera, microphone or precise location access. It does not send advertising requests in this version. Its Google Play listing may carry an advertising declaration; this does not mean that advertising data is collected by the current build. We will update this policy and the store disclosures before enabling data-processing advertising or online services.

If you choose to open this policy or contact us, your browser, GitHub Pages and email provider process the connection or message under their own policies. GitHub may log technical information when serving this website. See GitHub's privacy statement. This website contains no developer-operated analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies.

Retention, deletion and requests

Game records remain in the app's device storage until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Starting a new game replaces the current board but keeps your profile and personal best. To remove the app's records, use Android Settings → Apps → Block Puzzle Arena → Storage → Clear data. Device backup or restore behavior, if available, is controlled by Android and your device settings.

There is no online account to delete in this version. We cannot remotely retrieve, restore or delete records stored only on your device. For any privacy request, email metinzr55@gmail.com. If you contact us, we receive the information you include in your email and use it to handle your request. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information. Support messages are retained only as needed to respond, resolve the matter and meet applicable obligations.

Security and children

The app uses the device's application storage. We do not claim that game files are encrypted or that device storage is immune to unauthorized access or loss. This version does not offer messaging, public user-generated content, purchases or gambling. It is intended for a general teen and adult audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13.

Changes

We will update the effective date when this policy changes. The behavior and permissions of the version installed on your device determine which features are available.
